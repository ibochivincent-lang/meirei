## 🌟 The Core Mission: Financial Inclusion for Everyone

> *"Yes, we are trying to include everyone in this project — both old and young. That is why we are approaching social media where billions of people already spend their daily lives. The mandate can be given from anywhere in plain words, and you get automated weekly progress updates by just giving the command. You are always in control."*

For decades, global investing has been locked behind cumbersome Wall Street brokerages, high minimum deposits, banking barriers, and steep foreign exchange fees. Web3 introduced tokenized equities, but replaced banking friction with cryptographic hurdles: intimidating DEX order books, browser extension popups, seed phrases, and gas token management.

**Meirei (命令 - "Command / Order") changes this paradigm completely.**

By meeting people directly inside everyday messaging applications (Telegram and WhatsApp) and on an intuitive Web Terminal, Meirei enables anyone — from an 18-year-old college student saving their first $20 to a 70-year-old grandparent protecting their retirement against domestic currency devaluation — to issue conversational investment mandates. 

- **No wallet extensions required to get started**: Users can bind their identity simply by confirming their public wallet address or email.
- **Natural language mandates**: Send commands like *"Put $50 into NVDAx and AAPLx monthly"* or *"Allocate 30% into AI chips and 70% USDG"*.
- **Automated weekly progress reports**: Receive concise portfolio digests, performance analytics, and rebalance history delivered straight to your chat.
- **You are always in control**: 1-word commands (`STOP`, `PAUSE`, `REPORT`, `FREEZE`) give you instant non-custodial command over your capital.

## 🔒 Non-Custodial Security & Smart Contract Architecture

- **Zero Private Key Storage**: The platform never generates, stores, or sees user private keys.
- **EIP-2612 Gasless Permits**: Users sign off-chain transfer approvals (`eth_signTypedData_v4`), eliminating separate approval transactions and gas costs.
- **EIP-712 Mandate Structuring**: All investment mandates are formatted as typed structured data, providing full human readability before signing.
- **On-Chain Policy Guard (`MandateSessionKeyValidator.sol`)**:
  - Enforces hard daily spending limits.
  - Enforces strict 100 bps (1.00%) maximum slippage ceilings.
  - Rejects any transaction attempting to trade unlisted tokens or memecoins.
- **Emergency Circuit Breaker (`/freeze` and `/unfreeze`)**:
  - Immediate account freezing stops all autonomous rebalancing in one click or command.
  - Verification utilizes constant-time comparison (`crypto.timingSafeEqual`) and 6-digit expiring OTP challenges.
