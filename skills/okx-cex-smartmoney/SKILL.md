# OKX CEX Smart Money Skill

Detects institutional capital flows, smart-money positioning, whale accumulation trends, and large-block swaps for tokenized equities on OKX X Layer.

## Capabilities
- **Smart Money Net Inflow**: Tracks institutional net buying vs. retail selling per asset.
- **Top-Trader Positioning**: Long/short and accumulation ratios among high-tier market participants.
- **Whale Accumulation Signals**: Real-time identification of large-order absorption and liquidity concentration in tokenized equities.

## Usage in Meirei Advanced Mode
Powers the Smart Money telemetry column in the Advanced Market Table and weights risk parameters in the AI Advisory Studio.
